Privacy Statement for WalkBetter

English

Last updated: October 4, 2026

WalkBetter does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your walking progress, check-in answers, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

WalkBetter does not ask you to create an account, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data.

If you subscribe to WalkBetter Premium, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Since WalkBetter does not collect any personal information, there is no data to access, delete, or transfer. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed from your device.

Apart from Apple's App Store, which processes any subscription purchase, WalkBetter does not integrate with any third-party services that would receive your data.

Français

Dernière mise à jour : 4 octobre 2026

Étant donné que WalkBetter ne collecte aucune information personnelle, il n'y a aucune donnée à consulter, supprimer ou transférer. Si vous supprimez l'application, toutes les données stockées localement sont effacées de votre appareil.
